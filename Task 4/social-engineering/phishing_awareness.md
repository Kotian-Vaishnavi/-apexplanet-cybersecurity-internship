# Phishing Awareness Training

## Common Signs of Phishing

1. Suspicious URLs
2. Poor grammar or spelling
3. Urgent requests for passwords
4. Fake login pages
5. Unexpected attachments or links

## Prevention Tips

- Verify website URLs carefully
- Never share passwords
- Use Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA)
- Avoid clicking suspicious links
- Report phishing emails immediately

## Conclusion

Social engineering attacks manipulate users psychologically.
Awareness training helps reduce phishing risks.
